## MediaFlow v562 Stable

### Library History: open and edit every changed title
- Each Library History entry now shows up to three of its changed titles as small chips: cover (or category icon), name and what changed (＋ added, ✎ edited, － deleted). Click a chip to edit that title.
- When a change touched more than three titles, a **More · N titles** button opens a popup with **every** title in that change: its cover, full name (never cut off), category, what changed and which fields, plus **Edit**. A search box filters long lists.
- Before, an entry showed at most five "Edit" buttons, and long names pushed some out of view, so titles past those couldn't be opened from Library History.
- The popup stays open while you edit, so you can go through the titles one after another. Esc or Close shuts it.
- Presentation only: Library History entries, Restore, XP and sync are unchanged.
- The MediaFlow app (5.0.0.562) has the same chips and More popup.
